    Add a book

    Files are copied into this app on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

    Settings

    Headphone and watch buttons

    Usually a double-press on headphones.

    Usually a triple-press on headphones.

    Skip buttons

    The round buttons in the player, and the lock screen when it shows skip buttons.

    What a bookmark captures

    Longer captures take longer to turn into quotes. Applies to new bookmarks.

    The paragraph you bookmarked is always included. Needs the book text; existing quotes update too.

    Trimming quotes

    When you let go of a handle, the quote's end settles on the nearest whole sentence. Off, it stays on the word you left it at.

    Citations

    {time} is replaced with the bookmark’s place in the audio.

    Preview

    Added under a quote when you copy or share it. Publisher and year come from About this book. Styles are plain text, so titles are not italic.

    Sounds and figures

    A short tone when you bookmark, skip from your headphones or watch, or reach a picture.

    When the reading reaches one in the book text, the app chimes and shows it. To keep track of the place, it listens to a few seconds of the book every few minutes. Locked, that needs “Find quotes while locked”.

    Lock screen

    Experimental. Only works while audio is playing, and if iOS runs short of memory it may close the app and stop playback.

    Appearance

    Accent color

    Used for the progress bar, buttons and highlights. Very light or dark picks are adjusted so they stay readable in both themes.

    Diagnostics

    A record of recent button presses and player events, kept on this device.
